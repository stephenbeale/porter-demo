🔒 Privacy

Porter and your data

Your paperwork stays on your phone. Personal details are blacked out on the phone before any page is sent to be read.

Last updated 1 November 2026. Porter is a beta made by Stephen Beale. Questions: tap 💬 Feedback in Porter.

In short

  • No account, no Porter database. Your history and kept pages are stored in this browser on your phone, not on a Porter server.
  • Redacted before sending. Your phone finds names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, card and account numbers and signatures, and blacks them out. You check the boxes before anything leaves the phone.
  • Read, not kept. Pages are sent to Anthropic's Claude to be read. Anthropic doesn't train its models on them. Porter's beta server, if you use it, passes them on without storing them.
  • No ads, no tracking, no cookies for analytics or advertising.

What stays on your phone

  • Your car history: jobs, dates, mileage, costs, garages, notes from the mechanic, reminders.
  • The redacted copies of pages you keep with each job.
  • Your settings, and your Anthropic API key if you use your own.

These live in your browser's storage (localStorage and IndexedDB). Clearing the site's data, or "Delete all history" in Porter, removes them. Use Back up to keep a copy. Porter also saves its own files so it can open without a connection; that copy holds no personal data.

What's sent, and to whom

WhoWhat, and when
Anthropic (Claude)Redacted pages, and anything you type for Porter to sort (notes from the mechanic, a description of the work), when you tap Process, Send or Save. With your own key they go straight from your phone; otherwise through Porter's beta server. Read under Anthropic's privacy policy and commercial terms, which don't allow training on them.
Porter beta server (Cloudflare)Only if you were invited. Passes pages and notes to Anthropic and returns the answer, without storing or logging them. It keeps: today's spend and lookup counts per tester (deleted after 2 days), your redaction feedback answers (name, yes/no, optional comment; deleted after 180 days), and, if you sign in with Google, checks your email against the invite list.
DVSA and DVLAYour registration number, only when you tap the MOT history lookup (through the beta server).
GoogleOnly if you choose: Sign in with Google (your name and email, to the beta server), Save to Google Drive (Porter can only see the files it creates), or dictation in Chrome (your voice is turned into text by Google).
jsDelivr and GitHub PagesPorter and its on-phone reading tools are downloaded from these. Like any website, they see your IP address and device type when they serve the files.

Things to know

  • Check the boxes. Redaction on the phone is good but not perfect. You see every box before sending, and Claude covers anything missed in what you keep. Anything missed before sending was seen by Claude.
  • Sharing is your choice. The sale summary, service book and exports include what you choose (registration, VIN, costs), never your personal details.
  • People in photos are spotted on the phone and those pages are never sent.
  • Feedback you send with 💬 Feedback goes by the app you choose (WhatsApp, email and so on) to whoever invited you.
  • Porter is for adults managing their own vehicles.

Your choices

  • Delete everything: History → "Delete all history", or clear this site's data in your browser.
  • Stop using the beta server: use your own API key in Settings. Ask whoever invited you to remove your invite or email.
  • Google: remove Porter's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
