Add Porter to your Home Screen

On iPhone, tap Share ⬆ then Add to Home Screen, and open Porter from there. Safari deletes a website's saved data after 7 days without a visit; Home Screen apps keep theirs.

The Home Screen app has its own storage. If you've already saved history here, tap Back up under "Your car's history" first, then Restore it in the app.

What do the box colours mean?

Red: redacted on your phone before sending. Purple: personal details your phone missed, so they were sent, and AI found them; they're covered now. Orange: numbers matching your details from another scan, such as the end of a phone number showing under a receipt.

    Set up Save to Google Drive (once)
      
    Google needs Porter to have its own client ID, made in your Google account. It's free and takes about 10 minutes:
    
      
      
        
    1. Open Google Cloud: new project, name it "Porter" and create it.
      2. 
        
    2. Turn on the Google Drive API for that project.
      3. 
        
    3. In Google Auth Platform, get started: app name "Porter", your email, audience External. Under Audience, add your Gmail address as a test user.
      4. 
        
    4. Under Clients, create a client: type Web application, authorised JavaScript origin https://stephenbeale.github.io.
      5. 
        
    5. Copy the Client ID (it ends in .apps.googleusercontent.com) and paste it here. It isn't a secret.
      6.
