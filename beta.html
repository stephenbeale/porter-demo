Welcome to the Porter beta

Porter organises your car's service paperwork in one place: invoices, MOTs, receipts and what the mechanic told you.

Snap or upload the paperwork and Porter reads it, builds a timeline of every job and what it cost, reminds you when the MOT is due, and makes a tidy service history book when you sell. Your phone redacts your personal details before anything is sent.

Open Porter

Tip: add it to your home screen from the browser menu so it's one tap away.

1. Get started (about 5 minutes)

  1. Get an Anthropic API key. This is how Porter reads your paperwork during the beta. Sign up at the Anthropic Console, add £5 of credit under Billing (a document costs about 4–7p, so that's 70 or more), then create a key called "Porter" and copy it.
  2. Paste it into Porter under Settings. It confirms straight away and tucks the box away.
  3. Add your first document: tap a big button on the home screen and photograph an invoice or MOT certificate, or choose images or a PDF.

Your key stays on your phone and only ever goes to Anthropic.

Invited with Google sign-in? Skip the key: open Porter, go to Settings and tap Sign in with Google with the account whose email you gave. Given an invite code instead? Just open the invite link.

2. What to try

  • Add a garage invoice with two or more pages
  • Add an MOT certificate
  • Add a card receipt, or a screenshot of a part you bought online
  • Check the redaction: is anything personal still showing?
  • Log some DIY work, or note what a mechanic told you (Mechanic tab)
  • Look through History: the timeline, Most recent work, Year by year
  • Add a date to your phone calendar from the Calendar tab
  • Make the service history book (History → Back up & export)
  • Split a job, move a page, or change what a job is (✏️ Edit this job)

3. Tell us what you think

Tap 💬 Feedback at the top of Porter whenever something is confusing, wrong or great. Your message goes to whoever invited you, via WhatsApp, email or whatever you choose. A screenshot helps too.

Please don't put personal details (names, addresses, card numbers) in feedback.

Good to know

  • Your data stays on your phone. History and kept pages live in this browser only. Use Back up in History now and then, or save everything to Google Drive.
  • Privacy: your phone blacks out names, addresses, phone numbers and card details before a page is sent. AI double-checks and covers anything missed in what you keep.
  • It's a beta. Things may change or break; newer versions appear automatically (Porter tells you when one is out).
  • Works best on a recent phone in Chrome, Brave, Safari or Edge.
